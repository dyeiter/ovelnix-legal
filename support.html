OVELNIX Support

Need help with OVELNIX? We're here to help with paycheck estimates, tax settings, earnings tracking, subscriptions, and more.

Contact Support

Email us at support.ovelnix@gmail.com .

When contacting support, please include your app version, a description of the issue, and relevant screenshots if helpful.

Please do not send Social Security numbers, passwords, financial account details, or other sensitive personal information.

Subscriptions

OVELNIX Premium subscriptions are managed through Apple's App Store. To manage or cancel your subscription, open iPhone Settings, tap your Apple Account, then select Subscriptions.

To restore an eligible purchase, use Restore Purchases inside OVELNIX.

About paycheck estimates

OVELNIX provides estimated payroll calculations for informational purposes. Actual employer withholding and take-home pay may differ.

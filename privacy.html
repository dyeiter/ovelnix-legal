Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026
Publisher: Derek Yeiter
Contact: support.ovelnix@gmail.com

OVELNIX provides paycheck estimates, shift and earnings tracking, payroll history, and optional subscriptions. This policy explains how information may be handled when you use OVELNIX.

Information you provide

OVELNIX lets you enter information including compensation, work hours, shifts, employers, deductions, benefits, geographic tax settings, tax elections, and payroll history. Such information may be sensitive. The app uses entered information to calculate estimates and display tracking and history features. Payroll records are designed to be stored on your device; information you explicitly share or export may leave your device.

Subscriptions and purchases

For Premium purchases, the relevant app store processes billing and RevenueCat supports subscription verification and access. OVELNIX does not require you to enter a full payment-card number into the app. RevenueCat may process a subscription-related app-user identifier, purchase information, and device or technical information to provide subscription services. Read RevenueCat’s privacy policy, Apple’s privacy policy, and Google’s privacy policy.

Advertising

The Free version may display banner ads delivered by Google AdMob. When ads are displayed, Google and its partners may process data such as advertising identifiers, device information, and information about ad interactions, according to applicable consent choices and their policies. Where required, the app may request advertising consent or make privacy choices available. Premium is intended to remove banner advertisements while subscription access is active and verified. See Google’s advertising information.

Exports and sharing

If you export or share a report, the file or information may become available to the recipients and services you select. You are responsible for reviewing the information you export and the destination you choose.

Storage, security, and backups

OVELNIX uses application storage on your device for payroll tracking. Device or operating-system backup services may retain copies, depending on your settings. We take data protection seriously, but no storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Retention and deletion

You can change or delete supported records using OVELNIX’s available controls. Uninstalling the app generally removes its on-device application data, but copies may remain in device backups or files you exported. App stores and service providers may retain subscription, purchase, advertising, and related records under their own policies and applicable law.

Children

OVELNIX is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly seek personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through a service associated with OVELNIX, contact us.

Your privacy choices and rights

Depending on your location, privacy laws may provide rights to access, correct, delete, or limit the use of certain personal information. You can use the app’s record-management tools for locally stored data and device-level settings for some advertising choices. For questions or privacy requests relating to OVELNIX, contact support.ovelnix@gmail.com. Requests involving app-store or third-party records may need to be directed to those providers.

Changes

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the app or legal requirements. The current version and effective date will be displayed on this page.
